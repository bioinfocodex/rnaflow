🩺 QC summary — is every sample fit to analyse?

FastQC only looks at the reads. This checks where they went: how many mapped, how many came from rRNA or mitochondria, and on the genome route how evenly they cover each gene. Everything is gathered into one MultiQC report with a PASS / WARN verdict per sample. Look at it before running DESeq2 — one bad sample can distort every result.
